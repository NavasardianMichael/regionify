# Google Analytics report — Sep 4 to Oct 1, 2026

Source: GA4 "Reports snapshot" export for the Regionify property, 28 days (Sep 4 – Oct 1, 2026). Prepared Oct 2, 2026. Live checks of the site and code on Oct 1–2 filled in what the export can't show.

## Summary

- **Only about 69 real people reached the app in four weeks.** The export shows 76 users; about 7 of them are probably you or data-center bots. At this volume zero sales is expected even if the funnel works. If 1 in 100 visitors bought, there would still be a 50% chance of selling nothing in this period.
- **The country pages are the only channel that works.**
  - 58 of 76 users (76%) came from links on the `/marketing/` microsite.
  - Almost all of them came from two sections: the hero button (30) and the showcase cards (22).
  - The call-to-action at the bottom of the page brought 1.
- **Social posts don't appear at all.** There are no Reddit, LinkedIn, Medium or YouTube visitors anywhere in the export. Homepage tracking still worked until at least Sep 23, so this isn't only the tracking bug described below.
- **Search sends almost nobody straight to the app.** 5 users came directly from Google or Bing (15 sessions).
- **People who arrive do engage.** They average 1 min 46 s and about 2.9 page views each, with a 47% bounce rate.
- **GA can't yet show where people drop off.** No conversion events are set up, and every app page shares one title. The homepage stopped reporting in late September, and the marketing site has no GA at all.

How to read this:

- **`marketing / …`** rows are clicks from the 190 country pages and the guides. Each link is tagged with the page section it sits in.
  - The hero, showcase and nav links open the editor (`/projects/new`).
  - The hero also has a "View plans" link to `/billing`. This export can't separate the two hero links; the _Session campaign_ dimension can (`generate_map` vs `see_plans`).
- **`embed / credit`** is the "Made with Regionify" credit on public embed pages. It accounts for 9 sessions but zero new users, so every click came from someone who had visited before. It's most likely you checking your own embeds.
- **`embed / badge`** is one genuine new user who found Regionify through someone's embedded map.
- **`(direct)`** covers typed URLs, bookmarks, and links without tracking tags. Your social posts all carry tracking tags, so they would appear under their own source, not here.

## Who the users are

- **Roughly half are in the US** (about 37 of 76). The rest are spread thinly across about 30 countries, which is typical of long-tail search traffic to country pages.
- **Probably not prospects:**
  - Yerevan (4 users) is most likely you and people you've shown the app to.
  - Ashburn, VA (2) and Boardman, OR (1) are major AWS data-center locations. Visits from there are usually bots, uptime checks and link previews.
- Internal traffic isn't filtered yet, so these users are counted in every number above.

## What this export can't see

1. **Visits that start on the homepage.**
   - Since nginx began serving `/`, `/about`, `/faq`, `/terms`, `/privacy` and `/refund` from Node in late September, the server's security policy blocks the GA script on those pages. The fix is to allow Google's domains in `server/src/app.ts`.
   - The export shows the effect: the homepage's own title ("Regionify — Choropleth Maps, Live Embeds & Animated Exports") has 1 view all month.
   - 31 of the 32 tracked links in the Reddit, Medium, LinkedIn and YouTube posts point at the homepage.
2. **Visitors who read a country page and leave.** The marketing site has no GA. Only the 58 people who clicked through to the app are visible, so the microsite's real reach and click-through rate are unknown.
3. **Which app pages people used.** Every page nginx serves directly has the same title, "Regionify — Choropleth map & data visualization tool", which accounts for 217 of 218 views. The editor, pricing and checkout pages can't be told apart in a title-based snapshot.
4. **Conversions.** No key events are configured, and the app sends only two events, `page_view` and `file_download`. Nothing records a sign-up, a saved project, a checkout start or a purchase.
5. **Your own visits.** Without an internal-traffic filter, your testing inflates users, sessions and engagement.

## What this means for sales

This data can't distinguish a broken funnel from an audience that is simply too small:

| If this share of visitors bought | Expected sales from about 69 visitors | Chance of zero sales |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------: | -------------------: |
| 0.5%                             |                                   0.3 |                  71% |
| 1%                               |                                   0.7 |                  50% |
| 2%                               |                                   1.4 |                  25% |

These conversion rates are illustrative assumptions, not figures from this data.

The volume problem, however, is certain. At 1%, about 500 real visitors a month are needed for 5 sales, roughly 7× today's traffic. Fix the funnel leaks, but growing traffic is what decides whether sales happen.

## Recommendations

### 1. Fix measurement before the next promotion push

- Allow `googletagmanager.com` and `google-analytics.com` in the server's security policy. This restores homepage tracking and social attribution.
- Add conversion events in the app, then mark them as key events in GA (Admin → Events):
  - `sign_up`
  - `project_saved`
  - `file_download`, with a "watermarked" parameter
  - `begin_checkout`
  - `purchase`, sent from the Paddle webhook so it can't be missed
- Add GA to the marketing site, so country-page visitors and click-through rate become visible.
- Filter your own traffic: Admin → Data streams → _Configure tag settings_ → _Define internal traffic_, then enable the filter under Admin → Data filters.

### 2. Invest in the channel that works: the country pages

- **Carry the country into the editor.** Hero links open `/projects/new` without the country, so a visitor from the France page lands on an empty editor. Passing the country and preselecting it removes a step for 76% of your users.
- **Fix the paid-feature showcase cards.** "Try it with the Explorer badge →" opens `/projects/new`, where SVG, GIF and MP4 are hidden from free users. Point these cards at `/billing`, or at an editor view that shows the feature as locked with an upgrade prompt.
- **Rework or move the bottom call-to-action.** Across 190 pages it brought 1 user, while the hero brought 30.
- **Find the pages that already rank.** In Search Console, see which country pages get impressions and clicks, and give those pages richer content first.

### 3. Treat social as unproven

- If any posts went out between Sep 4 and Sep 23, they brought no tracked visitors.
- Post again only after tracking is fixed.
- Until the security-policy fix is deployed, link posts to `/projects/new?utm_source=…` instead of the homepage, because that page is tracked today.

### 4. Re-run this report in four weeks

Use the reports below, with the date range set to "since launch" (early August) rather than GA's default of the last 28 days.
